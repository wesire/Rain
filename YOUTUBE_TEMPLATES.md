## General Tips for All Videos

- Dark blue/grey background
- Clear white text: "12 HOURS" or "RAIN SOUNDS"
- Rain droplets or water imagery
- Keep it simple and readable
- Use consistent branding across videos

### Category
- Music

### Playlists to Create

- Enable "Made for Kids: No" (so viewers can add to playlists)
- Add to relevant playlists immediately
- Schedule releases for evening time (when people search for sleep content)
- Engage with comments
- Create thumbnails with consistent style
- Consider creating end screen with more videos

- Ensure audio is original (which it is with this generator)
